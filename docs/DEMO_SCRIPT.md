# AgriSaarthi 3-5 Minute Demo Script (Hackathon Video Presentation)

## [0:00 - 0:45] The Problem & Landing Page
- **Visual**: Landing Page (`http://localhost:3000/`) with Indian farmer hero portrait.
- **Narrator**: "Smallholder farmers in India and across BRICS nations face unpredictable weather and declining soil health. Chatbots hallucinate dosages, and satellite data remains locked away. AgriSaarthi changes this by bringing satellite telemetry, soil health, weather forecasts, and Google Gemini into an actionable daily operating system."
- **Action**: Highlight the 6 intelligence cards and click **Get Started**.

## [0:45 - 1:45] Farmer Dashboard & My Farm
- **Visual**: Farmer Dashboard (`/app`) and My Farm (`/app/farm`).
- **Narrator**: "Welcome to Ramesh Kumar's farm in Udaipur, Rajasthan. Notice our 8 live overview cards: 28°C, 20% rain probability, and healthy crop indicators. In 'My Farm', Ramesh sees his 2.5-acre wheat plot with satellite boundary coordinates."
- **Action**: Toggle the language button to **हिंदी** to show real-time translation across all cards.

## [1:45 - 2:45] Satellite, Soil & Disease Doctor
- **Visual**: Satellite View (`/app/satellite`), Soil Health (`/app/soil`), and Crop Doctor (`/app/disease`).
- **Narrator**: "In Satellite View, we track Copernicus Sentinel-2 NDVI—currently 0.78 with a rising 4-month trend. In Soil Health, our deterministic engine scores his loamy soil at 74/100, identifying nitrogen as the limiting nutrient. In Crop Doctor, Ramesh uploads a photo of affected foliage—our multimodal model identifies Early Blight with high confidence and offers organic preventative steps."

## [2:45 - 3:45] Regenerative Plan & AI Saarthi
- **Visual**: Regenerative Plan (`/app/regenerative`) and AI Saarthi (`/app/assistant`).
- **Narrator**: "Our Regenerative Agriculture score stands at 73/100 across 5 transparent pillars. And whenever Ramesh has a question, he asks AI Saarthi in Hindi or English. Because Gemini receives structured farm context—crop, soil, satellite, and weather—it delivers precise, localized advice."

## [3:45 - 4:15] Global Knowledge Exchange (BRICS) & Conclusion
- **Visual**: Knowledge Exchange (`/app/knowledge`).
- **Narrator**: "Through the BRICS Knowledge Exchange, Ramesh can adopt water harvesting techniques from Rajasthan or cover-cropping insights from Brazil with clear adaptation guidelines. AgriSaarthi: Smarter Farming, Healthier Tomorrow."
